The audience insight

Start with the problem in the pocket.

Everyday carry is about carrying smarter. A wallet needs to feel slim without sacrificing the things people actually take with them.

The creative idea

Turn an exaggerated, overstuffed wallet into a compact everyday setup. Make the benefit visible before asking viewers to care about the product.

From concept to sequence

Open on a bulky pocket, empty the old wallet, then rebuild the carry with the Soft Card Holder. Fast unboxing, card organization and a key attachment turn features into a simple transformation.
