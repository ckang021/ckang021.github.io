The audience insight

Lead with the behavior. Let the case support it.

Foldable users were sharing comic-reading experiences. The strategy connected that community behavior to a practical kickstand use case.

The creative idea

Frame the foldable as a $2k comic book and let the absurdly specific use case make the product story feel native to TikTok.

From concept to sequence

Reveal the comic on the inner display, turn the phone around to show the kickstand, then demonstrate page flips and interactive dialogue bubbles.
