The audience insight

Make the design connection the hook.

The storyboard connects a Mac Mini enclosure to a recognizable desktop-computer silhouette, then uses installation details to make the product’s role clear.

The creative idea

Begin with the design inspiration, then move from the separate components to a complete desk setup. Let the build explain the product.

From concept to sequence

Compare the inspiration and design, reveal the two-piece enclosure, and highlight ventilation. Install the Mac Mini, place the setup on the desk, and show access to the power button before the monitor turns on.
