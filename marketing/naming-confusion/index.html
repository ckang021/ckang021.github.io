The audience insight

Give a confusing launch a familiar punchline.

The launch-planning brief identified confusion around the Fold lineup’s names and form factors as a conversation the audience could recognize.

The creative idea

Use a simple comparison carousel to make that confusion the joke. Let viewers join in or explain the naming while the case lineup stays part of the visual story.

From concept to sequence

Build a three-part comparison: establish the previous Fold, introduce the familiar-looking successor, then reveal the wider model with a “then what is this?” question. Keep the text short enough to follow across slides.
