The audience insight

Put protection into a real routine.

The research called out limited Galaxy Watch content and an opportunity to make the band and protection lineup visible to Samsung users.

The creative idea

Move beyond a product lineup. Show how protection and different band styles fit into the rhythm of a day.

From concept to sequence

Open with everyday bumps and worn protection. Replace the protector and band, then use scene changes to connect each band to an outdoor or office setting.
