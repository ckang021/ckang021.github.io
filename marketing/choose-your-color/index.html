The audience insight

Make a color choice feel personal.

The brief frames a new phone color as more than a specification: a choice people can connect to their own personality and debate with friends.

The creative idea

Give each color a recognizable persona. Invite viewers to find themselves in the carousel and choose the one that fits.

From concept to sequence

Introduce the color lineup, then pair each phone with short personality cues: the Burgundy early adopter, Glacier Blue color analyst, Deep Black believer, and Silver Apple purist. Keep the devices and cases visible within the joke.
