The audience insight

Make every reveal earn the next one.

The launch brief treats the new foldable as the starting point for a wider accessory story, pairing the device reveal with protection, MagFit accessories, and charging.

The creative idea

Build a tactile unboxing that moves from the new phone to a complete everyday setup. Use sound and transitions to connect each reveal.

From concept to sequence

Open with the device box and seal removal, then introduce detail shots and button ASMR. Apply screen and camera protection, reveal the hinge protection, transition through cases, and finish with accessories and charging.
