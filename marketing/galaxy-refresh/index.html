The audience insight

Make the first frame feel overdue.

The strategy identified an opportunity to balance Apple content with Samsung stories and show how accessories work together as an ecosystem.

The creative idea

Use a visibly worn setup as the hook, then build a satisfying phone refresh around the Galaxy conversation.

From concept to sequence

Remove the old protection, apply camera and screen protectors, then introduce the case and MagFit wallet. Finish with the stand in use, connecting the refresh to an everyday viewing moment.

The takeaway

What the result suggests.

The recorded engagement rate was 4.77% across 140,628 plays. The result gives a concrete benchmark for the refresh format, without attributing sales or audience growth to a single post.
