The audience insight

Turn a keynote moment into a shared reaction.

The brief identified anticipation around a dark iPhone colorway as an opportunity to join the audience’s launch-day conversation.

The creative idea

Borrow the familiar joke of calling an album a classic after hearing one second. Translate that instant enthusiasm into a reaction to the color reveal.

From concept to sequence

Start with the keynote playing on a laptop. At the mention of dark grey, close the laptop and cut immediately to posting an excited Instagram Story. Keep the reaction quick and recognizable.

The takeaway

What the result suggests.

The post recorded 95,174 plays and 4,317 engagements, with a 4.54% engagement rate. It shows how a focused community reaction can earn attention alongside more detailed product stories.
