The audience insight

Turn a background detail into the main story.

Viewers had asked about the organizer when it appeared in earlier watch content. The brief uses that curiosity as the starting point for a dedicated product story.

The creative idea

Answer a real audience question with a satisfying transformation: scattered bands become an organized watch-and-charging setup.

From concept to sequence

Open on a messy desk covered in watch bands, bring in the organizer, and unbox it with ASMR. Reveal the drawer, arrange the collection, install the charger, and demonstrate choosing a band before returning the watch to its stand.
