The audience insight

Make the launch conversation feel like an inside joke.

The brief spotted a familiar meme opportunity around a new home-device announcement: recreating the idea with an iPad people already recognize.

The creative idea

Turn “we have it at home” into an escalating photo carousel. Start with an improvised home display, show a practical stand setup, then push the joke into an oversized watch.

From concept to sequence

Open with an iPad taped to the wall and a zero-budget installation joke. Follow with the Thin Fit Pro and One Tap Easel stand in a kitchen setting, then finish with an iPad on a wrist as an absurdly large Apple Watch.
